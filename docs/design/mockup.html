Sunflower Guard — One Door

One screen, one action. The whole screen is the button: tap anywhere to play, tap again to stop. You press it whenever you want. The app never decides that for you.

Six tracks take turns across the season so the flock never learns the pattern. That rotation runs on its own, but the track chip at the bottom right is tappable: each tap steps to the next track, and one more tap past the sixth hands it back to automatic. Nothing to set up, no internet.

Light ground by day, dark after sundown, purely so the screen is readable in the light you are standing in. The tick strip along the bottom is the product in one graphic — fifteen minutes, seven scares, irregular gaps, silence the rest of the time.
